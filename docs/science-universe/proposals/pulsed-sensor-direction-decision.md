# Pulsed-sensor task direction: stop packaging

Independent read-only screening decision: STOP task proposal/packaging and live
evaluation on the currently established evidence. Retain the dataset findings as
research screening/data-quality regression evidence. No scientific acceptance.

Confirmed duplicate signal groups, condition counts and concentration-support
mismatches are useful audit results. Turning hash/group/set checks into a
machine-readable answer does not make a difficult scientific task. Demanding
the already-discovered anomaly list would privilege fixed answers; accepting
arbitrary validation designs lacks a sufficiently evidenced scientific scoring
criterion. Preventing exact payload leakage is necessary, but does not prove
independent acquisition or an appropriate deployment test.

Do not ask a solver to distinguish duplicate export, experimental dependence,
concentration extrapolation and physical model discrepancy from evidence that
cannot identify those causes. Do not wrap the current findings as a first task
merely to complete launch gates.

Reopening requires all of the following:

1. Verifiable experiment hierarchy: acquisition order, original runs, independent
   electrodes/batches and calibration-versus-validation relationships.
2. A concrete deployment/scientific decision with a measurable consequence of
   different defensible analysis designs.
3. Independent measured validation assets adequate for that decision, beyond
   splitting duplicate timepoints from the currently inventoried dataset.
4. A fair executable scoring contract for information use, grouping and measured
   outcomes that accepts equivalent solutions rather than a known anomaly list.

No claim is made that these assets do not exist. The single-ion raw asset and
full primary paper have not been inspected; they remain research leads, not
established proof of these prerequisites. This decision stops implementation on
current evidence, not all future source research. No authors were contacted.
